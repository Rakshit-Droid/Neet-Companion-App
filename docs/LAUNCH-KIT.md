# Launch kit

Prompts for generating posters, banners and social posts, plus captions.

Everything here is built on what the app actually does. Nothing claims a
predicted rank, an admission chance, or a guarantee — the product deliberately
refuses to show allotment percentages, and marketing that promises them would be
selling something we do not ship.

---

## 0. Paste this first

Most image and text models produce generic output because they are given a
generic brief. Paste this block **before** any prompt below, in the same message.

```
BRAND: NEET Companion
WHAT IT IS: A counselling companion for Indian NEET UG aspirants. It builds an
ordered choice-filling list from seven years of real MCC results — 604 medical
and dental colleges, 17,066 closing ranks and 43,132 round-level records from
2019 to 2025.
WHO IT IS FOR: Students aged 17–19 across India who have written NEET UG and are
about to face counselling for the first time, and their parents.
THE FEELING: Calm, exact, trustworthy. The opposite of a coaching-centre banner.
No stock photos of celebrating students, no confetti, no fake toppers, no
percentage badges, no "100% guaranteed".
COLOUR: Lime green #7CCF00 as the single accent, used sparingly. Near-black
#0A0A0A for text. White #FFFFFF or very light grey #F4F4F5 backgrounds. Deep
lime #3F6600 where lime needs to work as text. No gradients, no purple, no blue.
TYPE: A squared, technical display face for headlines (Quantico), a clean
neutral sans for everything else (Inter). Wide letter-spacing on small labels.
LOGO: A caduceus — winged staff with entwined serpents — rising from an open
book, beside the words NEET COMPANION.
DESIGN LANGUAGE: Flat. No drop shadows, no glassmorphism, no 3D. Generous white
space, one idea per surface, data presented plainly.
```

## 1. A note that will save you a re-run

**Image models cannot spell.** Any prompt asking for a headline will usually come
back with mangled letters. So every prompt below asks for a composition with
**deliberate empty space** where text goes, and you set the type afterwards in
Canva or Figma using the real fonts.

That is not a workaround. It is how the results end up looking designed rather
than generated.

## 3. Captions

Pick one per post. Each has the claim it is allowed to make.

### 3.1 Launch announcement

> Counselling is not a guessing game any more.
>
> NEET Companion builds your choice-filling list from seven years of real MCC
> results — 604 colleges, 17,066 closing ranks, every round from 2019 to 2025.
>
> Not a prediction. What actually happened.
>
> Link in bio.

### 3.2 The thing most students get wrong

> Most students fill their choice list wrong.
>
> They put the "safe" colleges first, thinking it improves their odds. It does
> the opposite. Counselling allots the **highest preference you clear** — so a
> college you want more should always sit above one you want less, whatever the
> cutoffs say.
>
> NEET Companion orders your list the way counselling actually works.

### 3.3 Why there are no percentages

> We could show you "83% chance of getting this seat."
>
> We won't. Cutoffs loosened every year from 2021 to 2024, then tightened
> sharply in 2025. Any percentage fitted to that is a number someone made up.
>
> Instead you get the real closing ranks, every round, and how many years of
> data each one rests on. Judge it yourself.

### 3.4 Round-by-round

> Round 1 closed. You didn't get it. Is it over?
>
> Usually not. Cutoffs loosen as rounds progress, and a seat that was out of
> reach in Round 1 is often within it by the stray round.
>
> NEET Companion shows every round separately — with the years behind each one,
> so you can see whether a call rests on seven years of evidence or one.

### 3.5 Watchlist

> Track the colleges you actually care about.
>
> Add a college and NEET Companion follows it round by round, showing what moved
> and what it means at your rank.
>
> 5 credits a week per college. Stop any time.

### 3.6 Referral

> Counselling is easier when your friends are doing it too.
>
> Share your code. When a friend signs up and buys their first pack, you both
> come out ahead — you get 50 credits, free.
>
> No payout on signups alone. Only when they actually buy.

### 3.7 Parent-facing

> To the parents reading this:
>
> Your child is about to make a decision worth the next five years, using a
> WhatsApp forward and a coaching centre's guess.
>
> NEET Companion uses seven years of official MCC counselling results. No
> promises, no predictions. Just what actually happened, laid out clearly.

### 3.8 Telangana teaser

> Telangana state quota is next.
>
> 85% of medical seats are filled by state counselling, not MCC — different
> categories, different rules, different cutoffs. We are building it properly
> rather than guessing.
>
> All India Quota is live now.

## 4. Hashtags

Keep to eight or fewer. More reads as spam.

**Core:** `#NEETUG` `#NEETCounselling` `#MCCCounselling` `#ChoiceFilling`
`#MBBSAdmission` `#NEET2026` `#MedicalCollege` `#NEETCompanion`

**Regional, when relevant:** `#TelanganaNEET` `#KNRUHS` `#StateQuota`

Do not use `#NEETTopper`, `#NEETMotivation` or similar. Wrong audience, wrong
promise, and it attracts the coaching-ad crowd rather than students choosing
colleges.

## 5. Rules for anything published

These are not style preferences. Getting them wrong is a legal and trust problem.

**Never claim or imply:**

- a predicted rank, a chance percentage, or an admission guarantee
- that using the app improves someone's rank
- that any college is "guaranteed" or "confirmed" at a given rank
- endorsement by MCC, NTA, KNRUHS or any counselling authority

**Always true, safe to say:**

- built on official MCC counselling results, 2019 to 2025
- 604 colleges, 17,066 closing ranks, 43,132 round-level records
- All India Quota only; state quota is not covered yet
- orders a choice list by preference, because counselling allots the highest
  preference you clear
- shows the sample size behind every figure

**Audience is largely 17–18, which is legally a child under India's DPDP Act
2023.** Avoid manufactured urgency ("only 4 hours left"), fear framing ("your
career is over if…"), and anything that pressures a purchase. Behavioural
advertising to minors is restricted, and this audience is under real stress
already.

**Every price must be complete.** If a post mentions credits, it says what they
cost and what they buy: ₹100 for 50 credits, a choice list costs 2, watching a
college costs 5 a week. Ten free on signup. Partial pricing in an ad is what
gets a payments account reviewed.
