## 3. Captions

### 3.1 Why pay when the predictors are free

> Fair question. Our own predictors are free on neetcompanion.com and they always
> will be — put in a rank, see which colleges are in range.
>
> That tells you what you *could* get. It does not tell you what order to fill.
>
> Counselling allots the highest preference you clear. So the order of your list
> decides your seat, not the length of it. Getting that order wrong is how
> students clear a good college and still land somewhere they did not want.
>
> The app builds the order. That is the part worth paying for.

### 3.2 The list with no safe seat

> The most dangerous choice list is the one where every option is a stretch.
>
> Fill twenty aspirational colleges and clear none of them, and you do not get a
> worse seat — you get no seat. Then you are waiting for a later round with
> fewer options and less time.
>
> NEET Companion warns you when your list has no anchor: no college in it that
> your rank comfortably clears. It is the one warning we make impossible to miss.

### 3.3 What happened in 2025

> Cutoffs got easier every year from 2021 to 2024.
>
> Then in 2025 they tightened — the median closing rank moved back by about 5.5%.
> Students working from "last year's cutoff plus a bit" were aiming at a target
> that had moved the other way.
>
> This is exactly why we show you seven years, not one. A single year is a
> snapshot. Seven is a pattern — including the year the pattern broke.

### 3.4 One year is not evidence

> Some closing ranks in our data rest on seven years of results. Some rest on one.
>
> Those are not the same thing, and most tools show them identically.
>
> NEET Companion tells you which is which, on every round. When a number comes
> from a single year, you will know before you build a plan around it.

### 3.5 Your list, your priorities

> "Best college" means different things to different people.
>
> Some students want the highest-ranked institution, full stop. Some need to stay
> near home. Some will only consider government colleges because of the fees.
>
> Tell the app which you are and the whole list reorders around it. Same data,
> your priorities.

### 3.6 Last year's cutoff is not your cutoff

> The number circulating in your WhatsApp group is one year of one round of one
> quota.
>
> Cutoffs move between years, between rounds, and between quotas. A college that
> closed at one rank in Round 1 often closes far later by the stray round.
>
> Look at all of it, or you are planning around a fragment.

### 3.7 Where the data comes from

> No scraping guesses. No crowd-sourced spreadsheets.
>
> 604 colleges. 17,066 closing ranks. 43,132 round-level records with seat
> counts, from official MCC counselling results, 2019 through 2025.
>
> All of it ships inside the app, so your choice list builds instantly and works
> on bad network.

### 3.8 Credits, plainly

> No subscription. No auto-renewing trap.
>
> ₹100 buys 50 credits. A choice list costs 2. Watching a college costs 5 a week
> and stops on its own if you run out — nothing is deleted, it just pauses.
>
> You start with 10 credits free. That is five choice lists before you pay
> anything.

## 4. Replies you will need today

Day 2 is when the comments start. Write these once and reuse.

**"Is this official / MCC approved?"**
> No, and we would not claim to be. We use MCC's published counselling results —
> the same public data — and we are not affiliated with MCC or NTA. Always verify
> against mcc.nic.in before you act on anything.

**"Can you guarantee I get X college?"**
> Nobody can, and anyone who says otherwise is selling something. Cutoffs shift
> every year, and 2025 shifted the opposite way to the four years before it. We
> show you what actually happened so you can decide — not a promise we cannot
> keep.

**"Why not just show a percentage chance?"**
> Because it would be made up. Year-on-year drift went +10.5%, +4.4%, +3.3%,
> +1.3%, then −5.5%. No honest model fits that. We tested one, it failed its own
> reliability check, and we did not ship it.

**"Is my data safe?"**
> Your rank, category and course stay on your device. Your account is handled by
> Firebase, so we never see your password. We do not sell anything to anyone.

**"State quota?"**
> Telangana is next. State counselling is run separately by each state with
> different categories and local-candidate rules — we would rather build it
> properly than guess. Everything live today is All India Quota.

## 5. What not to post today

- No countdown timers or "offer ends tonight". The audience is largely 17–18 and
  already under real pressure; manufactured urgency aimed at minors is both ugly
  and restricted under the DPDP Act.
- No screenshots showing a real student's rank or name.
- No comparison naming a competitor. Compare against *guessing*, which is what
  you are actually replacing.
- No "trusted by thousands" until it is true and you can show the number.
